Zero plus anything
is a world

Continuing my contemplation of impermanence, interconnectedness and no self, these works are an exploration of how form, existence and meaning emerge. Our reality is constantly generated by the arising, abiding and ceasing of phenomena. Even the slightest addition or change generates a new reality, a different presence, a world that is constantly in flux.

When this is, that is
This arising, that arises
When this is not, that is not
This ceasing, that ceases

These works were created in response to 'pareidolia', my tendency to perceive patterns, shapes, especially faces on myriad surfaces in random or accidental arrangements of shifting shapes and lines. The seemingly chaotic juxtaposition of bodies in the paintings, the push and pull of shapes, scale, and perspectives all speak of this.
